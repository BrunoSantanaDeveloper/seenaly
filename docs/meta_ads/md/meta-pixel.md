Today, we're announcing updates to the Meta Pixel and Conversions API that will make it easier for any business to maintain the kind of well-connected, up-to-date setup that drives strong ad performance.

Providing the right information to enable ads growth has historically required technical expertise, such as frequently updating the code on your website, configuring servers, and updating product details manually. For businesses with leaner teams, especially smaller businesses, this complexity can be a barrier to getting the most out of their ads.

Our goal is to remove those barriers, and allow businesses to have the type of setup that top-performing advertisers rely on, without complicated onboarding, added costs, or ongoing technical maintenance.

### Your Meta Pixel, Upgraded

Setting up the Meta Pixel has long been an important step for advertisers. It helps Meta understand what actions people take on your website after seeing your ads, and it provides context needed to optimize your campaigns.

We're introducing a new Meta Pixel feature that allows businesses to use AI to automatically include additional page and product information – such as product names, availability, and business details – with the events you share with Meta. This is information many advertisers already share with Meta to run product-based ads, and we're simply helping automate what used to be a manual, technical process.

Sharing more context about your business and products can help our ads system better deliver the right ads to the right people, but maintaining detailed page and product information often required a dedicated developer team. This meant that advertisers with more resources, or fewer competing priorities, potentially had a performance edge.

Now, advertisers can configure the Meta Pixel to do this work for them automatically. This means smaller businesses can get the performance benefits, while reducing the need for constant and potentially complex technical work whenever they update their products or website. And larger advertisers can redeploy developer and technical resources to areas of their business that matter most to them.

Advertisers remain in control with this new product update. Existing Meta Pixel users will receive a notification with a 30-day window to review the feature before it's enabled. Advertisers can adjust or turn it off at any time through Events Manager, and they can also review and manage which specific data categories are shared so that they can optimize best for their business and compliance needs. Access may not be available to advertisers that have certain data source categories . As a reminder, advertisers are responsible for complying with the Meta Business Tools terms when using the Pixel, which includes ensuring that sensitive data is not shared with Meta.

### A Simpler Way to Set Up Conversions API

We have long recommended advertisers pair the Meta Pixel with our Conversions API, and advertisers with a Conversions API setup for web events saw an average 17.8% lower cost per result compared to those without Conversions API.

So with this in mind, we're also making it significantly easier to set up the Conversions API for web, with the introduction of a Meta-enabled Conversions API setup – a one-click option that requires no technical expertise, no costs, no ongoing maintenance, and will allow businesses to get set up in minutes.

If you're already using a partner integration or have a custom Conversions API setup, nothing changes. But if you have low event coverage, or if you've simply been waiting for an easier way to get started, that is less resource-heavy and technically demanding, implementing this new setup can help.

### More Complete Data, Better Performance

When advertisers can connect and securely share their business and product information, it helps Meta show the right products to the right people at the right time. We believe the best tools for growing your business should be accessible to everyone, and even advertisers with large technical teams will benefit from freeing up resources so they can focus on what’s important to them.

Check out the new features and start driving better performance today in Events Manager.
